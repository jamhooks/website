# JamHooks Coral & Deep Teal | Corrected brandkit

This pack traces the logo and fish-hook S from the user-selected Coral & Deep Teal reference, not the previous altered hook reconstruction. The S hook has an eyelet, inward-facing barb, and coral sweep.

Colors: Coral #FF5252, Deep Teal #006B6B, Dark Teal #004D50.

- `logo-primary.svg` / `.png`: full logo on light backgrounds.
- `logo-on-dark.svg` / `.png`: white & coral version for dark teal backgrounds.
- `icon-transparent.svg`: standalone fish-hook S and coral arc.
- `icon-on-teal.svg`, `icon-on-white.svg`: app icon.
- `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`: website icons.
- `icon-...-SIZE.png`: useful sizes from 16 to 1024 px.

Vectors are traced from the approved raster mockup and may have minor pixel-tracing imperfections.
